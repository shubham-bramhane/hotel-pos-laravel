End-to-End Workflow

From Guest Order to Printed GST Bill in 6 Zero-Paper Steps

Real Restaurant Flow
1

Waiter Opens Table

Waiter logs in with 4-digit PIN (4444) on their phone, taps empty green table T4, and selects guests.

2

Fast Order & Send KOT

Types short code (PBM, BN), adds note ("Less spicy"), and taps Send KOT. Table T4 turns Running Amber.

3

Kitchen Chime & Cook

Within 3 seconds, KOT-001 pops up on Kitchen Screen (3333) with audio beep. Chef taps Cooking → Ready.

4

Food Ready & Served

Waiter sees pulsing green READY badge, picks up hot food, serves table, and can send repeat rounds (KOT #2, #3) anytime.

5

Bill Request & Settlement

Waiter taps Request Bill. Cashier (2222) sees Blue alert, checks CGST/SGST, settles Cash / UPI / Split, prints 80mm receipt.

6

Owner Live Sales & Audit

Table frees automatically. Owner (1111) sees real-time Cash/UPI totals, top dishes, and every cancel/discount logged with staff name!
